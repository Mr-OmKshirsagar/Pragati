# PRAGATI — PRODUCT VISION

## 1. Vision

Build a system where a student's career development is continuously recorded, evidenced, evaluated and acted upon rather than reconstructed manually during placement season.

## 3. Product Differentiators

### Evidence-first records

Claims have verification states and supporting evidence.

### Explainable intelligence

Rules detect conditions.

AI explains and drafts.

Humans decide.

### Closed-loop intervention

Flag → action → outcome → resolution.

### Transparent eligibility

Students can understand exactly why they are or aren't eligible.

### Portable Career Passport

Student receives a structured career record that can be exported/shared.

## 6. Success Definition

A successful implementation allows a judge to follow one student's journey from:

Assessment
→ Skill Gap
→ Faculty Intervention
→ Improvement
→ Internship Evidence
→ Verification
→ Placement Eligibility
→ Recruitment Application.

The complete workflow must use real backend APIs and database records.
